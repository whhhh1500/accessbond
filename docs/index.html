AccessBond

Accessibility bounties, paid only when GenLayer validators see the fix live.

How it works

  1. Sponsor escrows GEN and picks criteria for a public URL. Validators fetch the page and record what fails today.
  2. Hunter fixes the site and claims. Validators re-fetch, re-check, and agree on a pro-rata payout (fixed − regressions).
  3. Sponsor may dispute once in the challenge window: an independent re-evaluation that weighs the sponsor's reason.
  4. Anyone finalizes: hunter is paid, the rest is refunded to the sponsor.
